# Softify v3.0.7: Official Release 🚀

Welcome to **Softify v3.0.7**! This major update brings a fresh Apple Music style player, living motion album covers, cinematic blurred lyrics, sleek liquid glass effects, hassle-free updates without security warnings, and smoother everyday performance.

---

## 🌟 What's New in Simple Words

### 🍎 Apple Music Player & Control Dock
- **A Fresh New Look**: Choose between Softify's Classic player and a brand-new modern **Apple Music style player**.
- **Intuitive Bottom Dock**: Easily switch between your song artwork, synchronized lyrics, and up-next queue from a convenient floating bottom dock.
- **Quick Volume Bar**: Adjust listening volume right from the player without reaching for your device's physical buttons.
- **Your Choice**: Switch styles anytime in **Settings > Appearance > Now Playing Style**.

### 🎬 Living Motion Album Covers
- **Moving Artwork**: Album covers now come to life with smooth, high-definition video animations while your music plays.
- **Zero Account Needed**: Works right out of the box on supported tracks with no login or subscription required.
- **Smart Data Saving**: Automatically caches motion covers locally so your mobile data stays light. You can also turn this off anytime in **Settings > Appearance > Motion Album Covers**.

### 🎤 Cinematic Depth-of-Field Lyrics
- **Sing Along in Focus**: The lyrics screen now gently blurs previous and upcoming lines with a natural optical effect, keeping your active line crystal clear.
- **Top-Line Tracking**: Lyrics automatically align smoothly toward the top of your screen so you always know what words are coming next.
- **Smooth on All Phones**: Optimized to run silky smooth on every device without battery drain. Choose between Classic and Apple Music lyric styles in **Settings > Appearance > Synchronized Lyrics Style**.

### 💧 Liquid Glass Design
- **Frosted Glass Aesthetic**: Experience translucent surfaces with dynamic ambient lighting, glassy borders, and natural reflections.
- **Available Everywhere**: Designed to look stunning across both mobile and desktop screens. Toggle it anytime via **Settings > Appearance > Aurora Glass**.

### 🛡️ Smooth & Safe App Updates
- **Goodbye False Alarms**: Completely eliminated annoying "Play Protect" security warning screens when installing updates.
- **One-Tap Updates**: Get new features faster and safer with seamless background downloads directly from GitHub releases.
- **In-App Update Banner**: Whenever a new update arrives, Softify greets you with a friendly summary of what's new.

### ⚡ Faster, Smoother & More Reliable
- **Seamless Song Crossfades**: Songs transition smoothly without micro-stutters or pauses.
- **Snappier Search**: Instant track and artist search suggestions pop up as fast as you type.
- **Bug Fixes**: Dozens of small stability improvements and performance optimizations across the entire app.
