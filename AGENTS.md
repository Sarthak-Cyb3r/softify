## 👑 Command Hierarchy

In this repository, **Reverse-Engineer-Anything** (`reverse-engineer-anything`), **AgentsOrchestrator** (`agency-agents-orchestrator`), and the **Chief of Staff** (`agency-chief-of-staff`) hold **supreme operational and architectural authority** over all automated pipelines, task delegations, and code reviews:

1. **Reverse-Engineer-Anything (`reverse-engineer-anything`)**: Supreme Architectural & Intelligence Authority. Dissects external reference apps (SimpMusic, Spotify), establishes ground-truth invariants, defines architectural blueprints, and oversees implementation fidelity.
2. **Chief of Staff / Agents Orchestrator (`agency-agents-orchestrator`, `agency-chief-of-staff`)**: Supreme Pipeline & Operational Leader. Enforces sprint schedules, directs subagent workflows, enforces QA gates, and coordinates commits.
3. **Specialist Implementation Agents** (`agency-senior-developer`, `agency-backend-architect`, `agency-frontend-developer`, `agency-mobile-app-builder`):
   - Subordinate to the Supreme Authorities.
   - Must implement strictly within the scope of assigned tasks.
   - Prohibited from scope creep or unauthorized architectural rewrites.
   - Must return concise completion reports with exact clickable file links (`file://...`) and verification steps.
4. **Auditing & QA Agents** (`agency-evidence-collector`, `agency-reality-checker`, `agency-code-reviewer`):
   - Provide independent, unvarnished verification evidence to the Supreme Authorities.
   - Enforce zero-tolerance for fantasy approvals or unverified claims.
